# Vedika-enterprises
Website of Vedika Enterprises

Business Details
Vedika Enterprises is a business specializing in water and wastewater treatment solutions. It offers
services like Boiler/Cooling Water Treatment Chemicals, STP/ETP Plants, Water Treatment Plants,
DM & RO Plants, Swimming Pool Filtration, Mineral Water Bottling, ETP Chemicals, and Pharma
RO/EDI Plants.

Website Goals
The primary goal of the website is to showcase the range of services offered by Vedika Enterprises,
highlight key clients, and provide a convenient way for potential customers to access company
information, including a downloadable brochure and contact details.

Design and Tool Decisions
The website uses HTML5, CSS3, and Bootstrap 5 for responsive layout and styling. Custom fonts
and icons from Google Fonts and Bootstrap Icons enhance the visual appeal. Animations and
auto-scrolling sections improve user engagement. The design is clean, modern, and mobile-friendly.
